Every family carries stories worth keeping — a voice, a memory, the exact way someone told a joke. Too often, they live only in the people who remember them, and fade when they do. Anvay is a home for those stories, built so they can be found, added to, and passed on.

The name originates from an old Sanskrit word meaning connection — the thread that runs between generations, joining who came before to who comes next. That thread is what this place is for.

Inside, there are two ways to keep it going. A living Family Portal — video interviews, photos, a family tree, letters written for someone's future — that grows for as long as the family does. And Legacy Books: a complete, written life story for one person or one couple, woven from their own words and the memories of everyone who loved them.
