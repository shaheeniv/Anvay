Every family carries stories worth keeping: a voice, a memory, the exact way someone told a joke. The moments that shaped us often live only in the people who remember them, and when those voices are gone, pieces of our story disappear with them.

Anvay is the world's first family legacy platform. It is a place to preserve the stories, memories and traditions that make a family unique. Built to be discovered, added to and passed forward, Anvay creates a living connection between generations.

The name originates from an old Sanskrit word meaning connection. It represents the thread that runs between generations, linking those who came before and those who come next. That thread is what Anvay exists to protect.

Inside, there are two ways to keep your family story alive.

The Living Family Portal brings together video interviews, photographs, family trees, traditions and letters written for the future, creating an evolving archive that grows for as long as your family does.

Legacy Books are beautifully crafted written life stories for one person or one couple, woven from their own words and the memories of everyone who knows and loves them.

Together, they create more than a record of the past. They create a legacy for the generations still to come.
